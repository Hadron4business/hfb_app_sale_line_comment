Customer Comment per Order Line

Let shoppers add a note to a product right in the cart - engraving text, name to print, prescription number - and keep it on the order, invoice and delivery.

The problem

Some products need one more piece of information from the customer. Odoo has no place for it on the cart line, so customers put it in a general order note (if at all) and somebody has to match it to the right product by hand.

What you get

Per product or category

Switch the box on for a single product or for a whole product category (subcategories included).

Your own label

"Engraving text", "Name on the shirt", "Prescription number" - translatable into every website language.

Saved as you type

No extra button: the comment is stored on the cart line while the shopper types.

Sales order and portal

Visible and editable on the order line in the backend, shown to the customer in the portal and on the printed quotation/order.

Invoice

Copied to the invoice line and printed on the invoice.

Delivery

Added to the description of the delivery move, so the warehouse sees it on the transfer and on the delivery slip.

How to use it

1
Open a product, tab Sales, section Extra Info: tick Ask for a Comment in the Cart and optionally set a Comment Label. Or do the same on a product category (Inventory/Sales > Configuration > Product Categories).
2
On the website, add the product to the cart: a comment box with your label appears under the product.
3
Confirm the order: the comment is on the order line (column Customer Comment), in the customer portal and on the printed order.
4
Create the invoice and validate the delivery: the comment is on the invoice line, on the printed invoice and on the delivery slip.

Good to know

  • The comment can also be typed or changed by your team on the order line in the backend.
  • The invoice keeps a copy of the comment from the moment it was created; the delivery shows the comment from the moment the order was confirmed (and the live value in the transfer list).
  • Comments are limited to 2000 characters.
